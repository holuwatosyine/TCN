## Sequential-pass additions

The preloader was inspected in a stable browser viewport at the completed numeric-loading state and visibly reported **Finalizing** before the forward gesture was completed. Forward wheel gestures then changed the status to **Entering**; reverse wheel behavior is now intentionally non-advancing.

The testimonial surface was corrected at its source from the earlier dark shader wrapper to a pale mineral/light environment with dark readable copy. The previous rounded object crossing a testimonial quote was isolated by an A/B pixel probe to the Passage canvas rather than the DOM testimonial markup. Passage now uses DOM-aware candidate placement and a conservative occlusion guard to keep the optical lens off readable copy where an open position exists and fade it rather than cover words when no safe position exists.

The headless mobile screenshot evidence remains valid for full Hero mounting and responsive canvas composition. It is not approval of final physical-device appearance: the browser environment uses emulated/software rendering, and a representative iPhone/Android pass is still required for perceived contrast, lens placement, GPU timing, thermal behavior, and context pressure.
